## Decisions that affect interpretation

- Pneumothorax uses an already reviewed chest image. It does not teach that routine ultrasound can establish a universal pneumothorax insertion site. Effusion specifically requires its ultrasound localization step.
- Vascular catheters travel through an enlarged route with named anatomical regions. The right arm route includes the brachial, axillary, subclavian and brachiocephalic segments; the femoral route includes iliac and successive aortic regions. These continuities were checked against [OpenStax circulatory pathways](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology/pages/20-5-circulatory-pathways). The guide is no longer a camera-facing line ending on the outside of a coronary artery or aortic cusp. This route is a teaching abstraction, without patient-specific fluoroscopy or CT sizing.
- The chest-wall workstation contains skin, fat, muscle, ribs, the intercostal bundle, pleural space, lung and diaphragm. Protected-region contacts can produce the same recorded preservation failure as contacts on the source specimen. The source and workstation share stable structure IDs but have separate geometry.
- Preparation alone cannot improve disease. Pneumothorax requires drain placement and a connected circuit; asthma requires delivery and inspiration; pneumonia requires prescribed medication; coronary and valve responses follow their respective device actions.
- Pneumonia improvement is labelled as days of recovery compressed. Oxygen does not clear the inflammatory filling. Valve replacement retains the native valve around a separate scaffold and replacement leaflets. Coronary plaque is displaced toward the wall rather than disappearing.
- Drug doses, oxygen-flow prescriptions, insertion depths, balloon pressures, device sizes, anticoagulation and monitoring thresholds are not generated from arbitrary values. The fictional case supplies an established plan. Controller distance/angle thresholds assess the enlarged model interaction and are not clinical limits.
- Patient-chart checking, sterility, local anaesthesia, imaging interpretation and follow-up currently use represented interactions. The scene does not yet model every dependency of real sterile technique, live imaging, drug delivery or tissue biomechanics. Keep these limitations in content review and release notes, rather than a wall of warnings over the playing area.

## Difficulty and progression content

Easy identification introduces at most three targets in order. Assembly uses the actual rest pose with a generous capture distance and angle; Medium and Hard narrow the acceptance region. Guided Dissection Easy is explicitly superficial preparation: skin, subcutaneous tissue and fascia. Medium extends into chest-wall muscle. Hard continues through the authored opening to deeper regional targets. A cut by itself does not earn exposure credit: the layer must be opened and moved clear so underlying source anatomy can be seen.

All variant IDs retain their existing mode/difficulty ordinals. The current content version is `2026.09.3`; historical `2026.09.1` and `2026.09.2` SQL definitions remain untouched. Objective IDs contain their action and target, so an old index-based sternum objective cannot be interpreted as a skin objective, or an artery-opening objective as external identification. Historical results and rewards remain available, while the updated curriculum requires its own Easy and Medium mastery. An incompatible checkpoint produces an explicit start-again message and its old actions remain unchanged.
