# Thoracic anatomy reference review

The museum catalog contains 249 entries. Of these, 243 represent tissue and six mark cavities or septal regions. The review below compares the shipped source records with atlas data and human specimen references. The project's professional-review packet lists approval as pending. [Structure catalog](../exports/anatomy-catalog.json) · [Professional review status](professional-validation/ANATOME-Professional-Validation.md)

## Findings for the museum and specimen

| Structure | Finding and implication |
| --- | --- |
| Cardiac valves | Eleven BodyParts3D meshes represent three tricuspid leaflets, two mitral leaflets, three pulmonary cusps and three aortic cusps. The original material generator gave all eleven the same finish as myocardium. The museum and lab now assign a separate connective-tissue surface and cut face to those four valve groups. Human donor specimens support the distinction between a valve leaflet and adjacent muscle. Their exact surface color and gloss under gallery lights are artistic choices. [Material generator](../unity/ThoraxLab/Assets/ThoraxLab/Editor/VRSpecimenAssets.cs) · [Human valve photographs](https://www.vhlab.umn.edu/atlas/comparative-anatomy-tutorial/valves.shtml) · [Histology of 50 human hearts](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32727956/) |
| Transversus thoracis | `FMA9761` and `FMA9762` are the right and left transversus thoracis. The original shared museum description placed them between the ribs. Both labels now describe the muscle on the deep anterior chest wall, extending from the posterior sternum toward the costal cartilages, typically 2–6. [Museum descriptions](../unity/ThoraxLab/Assets/ThoraxLab/Learning/Core/MuseumCollection.cs) · [Texas Tech gross anatomy tables](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/cardiovascular_system/thorax_wall_tables.html) |
| Lung lobes | `MAL_RUL`, `MAL_RML`, `MAL_RLL`, `MAL_LUL` and `MAL_LLL` preserve the source names and the three-right, two-left arrangement. MAL means **Mauritanian Anatomy Laboratory**. Its lobes come from one CT dataset. The production model fits all five to a different donor's thorax. Museum selection now displays the full original CT segmentation for each lobe. Its plaque identifies the lab’s cross-donor assembly. [Atlas publisher](https://www.openanatomy.org/atlas-pages/atlas-mauritania-thorax.html) · [Registration record](lung-registration.json) · [Brown University lung dissection](https://sites.google.com/brown.edu/ims-i-anatomy-lab/thorax-lab-guide/thorax-lab-2a) |
| Pleura and pericardium | The four `REC_*_Pleura` meshes are expanded convex envelopes of each lung's lobes. The `REC_Pericardium` sac is an envelope around the heart walls. These constructions simplify fissures, hilar reflections and the relationship of the sac to the great vessels. The revised museum plaques identify these teaching reconstructions. Anatomically, visceral pleura follows the lung surface and meets parietal pleura at the hilum. [Construction code](../pipeline/build_anatomy.py) · [Texas Tech thoracic dissection](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/cardiovascular_system/thorax_wall.html) |
| Chamber and septal markers | Four chamber-cavity contours represent spaces. Two septal markers identify regions within source walls. The museum and lab continue to identify them as reference annotations. The separately modeled valve pieces and papillary muscles represent actual tissue. ## Surface interpretation

- Pulmonary arteries carry oxygen-poor blood from the right ventricle. Pulmonary veins return oxygenated blood to the left atrium. The revised vessel-wall materials use illustrative tissue hues. The museum descriptions give flow direction and oxygenation explicitly. [NHLBI blood-flow reference](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/heart/blood-flow)
- Costal cartilage is hyaline cartilage. The trachea has incomplete cartilaginous supports with posterior trachealis muscle. A repeating ring texture around the complete circumference would teach the wrong anatomy. The catalog describes the tracheal lumen wall as an authored offset of a source cast. [University of Basel rib specimen](https://tales.nmc.unibas.ch/en/histology-12/knorpel-allgemeine-histologie-41/hyaline-cartilage-rib-241) · [Duke human trachea slide](https://histology.oit.duke.edu/NormalBody/Respiratory/Respiratory.html) · [Structure catalog](../exports/anatomy-catalog.json)

BodyParts3D began with MRI and incorporated expert modeling. Its digital surfaces do not establish patient-specific albedo or microscopic architecture. The NLM Visible Human Project supplies cryosections, CT and MRI for comparison. The local attribution records consultation of its documentation only. This review establishes source identity and gross anatomical distinctions. Specimen-specific fidelity and educational use still require professional review.
