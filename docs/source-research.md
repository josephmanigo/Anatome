# Thorax source acquisition and inventory audit

The primary source is the current official BodyParts3D 4.0 IS-A OBJ archive. Its license page explicitly specifies CC BY 4.0 and was updated on February 27, 2025. The archive is documented as a **99% polygon reduction** export. Its geometry is a starting point for asset work, not evidence that the full requested teaching inventory is present. [Official license](https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/lic.html), [official download](https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html).

Required attribution: **BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science licensed under CC Attribution 4.0 International**. Preserve that attribution with exports and record every modification. Original archive bytes and metadata are retained under `source/bodyparts3d`; `source/source-manifest.json` records source URLs, SHA256 hashes, byte sizes, and verification times. No Z-Anatomy or earlier ANATOME geometry is included in this acquisition.

## Reproducible selection

Run `pipeline/acquire_sources.py` with Blender's bundled Python to acquire and CRC-check the archive, then `source/generate-selection.py` to produce `source/thorax-selection.json`. The inventory generator resolves named concepts through the official elemental-ID tables. It gives specific IS-A identities precedence over PART-OF assemblies and assigns each elemental OBJ only once.

The selection contains **152 teaching records backed by 488 unique elemental IDs**. Its `missing_groups` field explicitly records **14 incomplete groups**. Its per-element geometry section includes vertex/triangle counts, bounds, and SHA256. These are source-selection records; completed authored reconstructions must have their own provenance entries. The complete original archive is 142,903,898 bytes, SHA256 `40665852c49f218326590e204db91064a1ecfc3c6f8cbd7bbbcaac62c7cd409e`, with 2,234 extracted OBJ files and valid ZIP CRCs.

The official coordinate diagram establishes **millimetres**, positive X toward anatomical left, positive Y toward posterior, and positive Z toward superior. A rigid supine Blender mapping is `(X, Z - regional_center_Z, -Y) * 0.001`; placement on a table is a separate translation. Use one shared origin and scale for all source structures. [Official coordinate diagram](https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/data/bodyparts3d/LATEST/coordinate_system.png).

## Coverage findings that affect modeling

The 4.0 release notes explicitly describe mapping confusion in segmented lungs and identify errors in using this dataset as a canonical model. The following findings are reproduced from the downloaded tables; they are not inferred from a thumbnail. [Official release notes](https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/data/bodyparts3d/LATEST/release_4.0_e.html).

- All 24 individually named ribs, 14 named costal cartilages (1–7 on each side), the three sternum regions, T1–T12, 11 thoracic discs, bilateral clavicles/scapulae, pectoral regions, serratus anterior, three intercostal muscle groups, and diaphragm resolve to elemental mesh IDs.
- Lung-lobe PART-OF concepts contain airway and vascular elements; the published tables do not establish independent lung parenchymal surfaces. Do not rename those branch assemblies as finished lobe tissue. Lobe surfaces/fissures need a separately documented reconstruction or verified additional source.
- FJ2432 is assigned to a posterior mitral leaflet by IS-A metadata but also occurs under inferior ventricular-wall concepts in PART-OF metadata. FJ2418/FJ2429 are papillary-muscle elements reused in ventricular-wall compounds. The inventory preserves specific leaflet/papillary identities. A deeper audit of all 2,234 actual OBJ headers recovered **FJ2428 / FMA13884, wall of ventricle**, which the PART-OF heart compound omits. This real shared ventricular wall is now selected; it must not be replaced by a fabricated exterior. Individual wall/septum region segmentation remains unconfirmed.
- Four cavity elements have explicit source identities for right/left atrial and ventricular cavities. They are **reference-only**, with cutting disabled in the inventory. They must not be displayed as solid myocardium or used to pretend chamber walls exist.
- No requested vagus/phrenic/intercostal nerve, sympathetic-trunk, thoracic-duct, pleural, pericardial, thoracic-fat, or thoracic-fascia identity was found. Generic cranial nerves and limb fascia are not substitutes. The existing whole-body skin must be cropped for a thoracic specimen.
- Lower costal cartilage and complete numbered intercostal neurovascular-bundle coverage are unconfirmed. Inspect source rib terminations before adding separate cartilage, to avoid overlapping a region already represented by a rib mesh.

Missing anatomy requires an **authored reconstruction** label, cited visual references, and a review of the actual completed geometry. A smooth mesh or accurate label alone does not establish anatomical correctness. No professional sign-off is required by this project.

The original OBJ files retain historical CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan comments. The current archive's official license and README explicitly specify CC BY 4.0 following the 2025 update; both the unchanged historical bytes and current license evidence are preserved. Do not rewrite the original source headers.

The FJ2428 ventricular wall has 37,442 triangles and bounds in millimetres `[-23.0179,-176.605,1174.19]` to `[79.1396,-83.1177,1265.92]`. Twenty-five points confirmed inside each ventricular-cavity source mesh were all outside the wall according to signed winding, supporting retention of both sampled chamber voids. Exact-position welding in a diagnostic array eliminates raw split-vertex boundary edges but leaves seven nonmanifold edges; production topology cleanup remains necessary. `source/heart-source-geometry-audit.json` records numerical evidence, and `source/audit-heart-geometry.py` reproduces it without editing source geometry. These samples do not certify every wall junction or valve continuity.

## Independent references retained

**Gray plate 806** was acquired as the original 467 × 600 PNG. It shows phrenic/vagus relationships and branches around the thoracic viscera. **Gray plate 968** was acquired as the original 700 × 550 PNG. It shows thoracic cross-sectional relationships among pleura, pericardium, airways, esophagus, vessels, vagus nerves, sympathetic trunks, and thoracic duct. Their Commons pages identify the scans as public domain. The images were opened and visually inspected. Diagram spacing is not a measured coordinate system. [Gray806 source and licensing](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gray806.png), [Gray968 source and licensing](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gray968.png).

**Open Anatomy MAL Thoracic Atlas** describes a CT-derived 38-year-old male specimen and links the permissive 3D Slicer Section B license. Its advertised downloadable ZIP returned HTTP 404, but a subsequent recovery followed the working viewer's default configuration to its live `atlasStructure.json`. **All five labeled lung-lobe VTK surfaces, 14 additional reference structures, and the authoritative segmentation NRRD are now downloaded**, with original coordinates and SHA256 records under `source/references/open-anatomy`. The five lobe surfaces total 135,634 triangles. [Atlas documentation](https://www.openanatomy.org/atlas-pages/atlas-mauritania-thorax.html), [live structure JSON](https://www.openanatomy.org/atlases/macbioidi/mauritania/thorax-2020-11-15/atlasStructure.json), [license](https://www.openanatomy.org/atlas-pages/slicer-license.html#PART-B).

These original VTK files use **RAS coordinates in millimetres**. The authoritative NRRD explicitly declares LPS, with spacing 0.644 × 0.644 × 0.80005 mm. Converting its five label bounds from LPS to RAS matches the VTK anatomy: most bounds agree within 0.23–0.50 mm, while the right middle lobe has a 2.06 mm maximum discrepancy and the right upper lobe posterior extreme differs by 7.61 mm. `coordinate-audit.json` records the numerical evidence and remaining discrepancies. Do not infer coordinate basis from the default used by a recent Slicer release: these historical viewer meshes contain no explicit SPACE header, so the actual label/mesh comparison establishes their basis. [Slicer file coordinate conventions](https://slicer.readthedocs.io/en/latest/user_guide/data_loading_and_saving.html).

The MAL meshes are a separate specimen. No registration is performed in source acquisition. A shared basis conversion to BodyParts3D uses `diag(-1,-1,+1)` before one common similarity/rigid transform. `registration-landmarks.json` supplies three corresponding sternum-region **area-weighted surface-centroid estimates** and explicitly distinguishes them from manually identified anatomical fiducials; their nearly collinear arrangement does not independently constrain all rotations. Preserve all lobes' relative positions, record the complete common transform and residuals, and inspect against the source skeleton and airways after registration. There is no MAL diaphragm model in the live atlas. Never normalize the five lobes independently.

The MAL manifest retains authors and the required Brigham and Women's Hospital notice. Include the complete Slicer Section B terms with redistributed portions and identify modifications clearly. `vtk_reader.py` reads the observed ASCII POLYDATA into arrays without changing source coordinates. The source NRRD and VTK files remain unchanged.

Original MAL lobe geometry has now been rendered from anterior, posterior, and left lateral viewpoints for source inspection (`lobes-*.png`). Fissure boundaries and independent lobe surfaces are visible. These are plain reference renders, not completed game materials. The topology audit found: right inferior lobe 0 boundary/0 nonmanifold edges; left inferior 0/1; right middle 4/2; left superior 2/1; right superior 2/2. No zero-area triangles were found. Repair these localized topology defects in derived production copies and preserve the original shapes; do not claim every raw lobe is already suitable for Boolean cutting.

**NLM Visible Human** is a public-domain cross-sectional image source. Its documentation is retained; image volumes have not been downloaded or segmented in this acquisition. Cross-sectional reconstruction would require an explicit segmentation and registration record. A link to its documentation must not be presented as a completed reference comparison. [NLM Visible Human](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/research/visible/visible_human.html).

Cached reference HTML pages are evidence files. Their page-text license is distinct from the referenced image/data license; do not bundle them as game art. Final art attribution should be generated from actual incorporated source records.
