# Hard heart dissection: preserve the heart and identify its exterior

Research checked on 6 September 2026 for the final Hard Guided Dissection heart objectives. This is an anatomy-laboratory teaching sequence, not a procedure for treating a living patient.

Texas Tech University Health Sciences Center El Paso's thoracic-wall laboratory manual separates opening the pericardial sac from examining the heart inside it. Its steps 10–11 first locate the phrenic nerves and associated vessels, then open the pericardial covering and inspect heart/great-vessel relationships. In-situ identification includes the right atrium and pulmonary trunk. This supports opening the covering while preserving the heart and nerves, followed by external identification objectives. [University thoracic wall, pleura and pericardium manual](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/schemes/thorax_wall.html)

The university's separate heart manual treats coronary examination as careful removal of epicardium and epicardial fat, followed by locating and tracing coronary arteries while avoiding superficial veins. Deliberate chamber opening appears later as a different exercise. A generic “open the right coronary artery” objective does not represent that coronary-identification task, and removing the right atrial wall merely to bypass an occluded target would silently change the dissection scope. [University heart laboratory manual](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/cardiovascular_system/heart.html)

Recommended final curriculum mapping, based on those sources and the installed catalog:

| Purpose | Catalog target | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Open the covering | `REC_Pericardium` — Pericardial sac | Verified cut and lift/reflect, followed by actual deeper-surface access. |
| Recognize heart exterior | `FMA9457` — wall of right atrium | Select the visible external wall; preserve it. |
| Recognize a great-vessel relationship | `FMA8612` — pulmonary trunk | Select; preserve it. |
| Preserve coronary supply | `FMA50039` / `FMA50040` — right/left coronary artery | Protected structures; no cutting objective. |
| Preserve phrenic nerves | `REC_Right_Phrenic_nerve` / `REC_Left_Phrenic_nerve` | Protected structures during pericardial work. |

The right atrial wall, pulmonary trunk and coronary arteries are existing BodyParts3D source meshes. The pericardial shell and enlarged phrenic paths are documented authored reconstructions; their thickness, diameter and exact clearance are not patient measurements. These are local catalog facts, not claims derived from the university diagrams.

Curriculum correctness and scene accessibility remain separate checks. The runtime must verify a real exposed surface after the covering moves, and accept identification without requiring another cut. It must not hide chamber walls or award exposure solely because the sequence advanced. No clinical-certification or independent professional-approval claim follows from this research.
