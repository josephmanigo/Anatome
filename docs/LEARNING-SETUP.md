## Learning and controls

The short orientation teaches selecting the skin with trigger, gripping it, and lifting/releasing it. The game restores the skin and opens **Easy**. A passed Easy assessment unlocks Medium for that unit/mode; a passed Medium assessment unlocks Hard. Passing requires at least 80%, every essential objective and no critical preservation failure. Lessons, practice and XP cannot bypass mastery. Retries are unlimited, with no mandatory timer.

Exactly four modes remain: **Identify & Enumerate**, **Assemble Anatomy**, **Guided Dissection** and **Anatomy Cases**. Free dissection is inside Guided Dissection. The activity panel shows one current step; tools, transcript and additional help use a separate page.

- **Select/use:** point the controller and press trigger. Actual tracked-device hits drive interaction.
- **Assemble:** grip, move/rotate and release. Easy stages one nearby correctly oriented piece with a real rest-pose outline and generous capture region. Medium and Hard require greater precision.
- **Dissect:** select the named layer, use scalpel for soft tissue or saw for bone, and hold trigger while drawing the opening. After the cut commits, grip the opened tissue or detached piece and lift it clear. A cut alone does not score exposure; the next layer must be physically accessible. Easy prepares skin, subcutaneous tissue and fascia; later difficulties extend deeper.
- **Cases and reasoning:** inspect the actual anatomy, then grip a mechanism cutaway or predicted response and release it in the patient-plan dock. There are three physical choices. Wrong choices explain the anatomical misconception. Guided help marks the correct model and dock without submitting evidence. Assessment and Hard steps offer alternative instruments. In the aspiration case, continuing after new chest discomfort is a critical safety error that correction cannot erase.
- **UI/help:** trigger operates buttons, stick scrolls a pointed-at panel, and gripping the top edge repositions it. **Tools and more help** contains tool switching, undo and restore. **Show me this step** is available during lessons/practice. Clinical choice labels follow saved text size and contrast, including the maximum text setting.
- **Settings:** Menu opens comfort, audio, learning/accessibility, graphics/display, controls, profiles and account/save categories. Apply/Cancel, category resets and named profiles remain. Risky Windows display changes revert after 15 seconds. Performance suggestions never silently change preferences.

Recorded assessment errors survive undo/restore. Geometric access checks use actual mesh surfaces and reject hidden targets. Finish closes the attempt without awarding missing objectives. Unlocks and checkpoint replay require matching curriculum versions: older history remains available, but it cannot establish mastery for 2026.10.1 or resume changed objectives.
