# Anatome

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
VR-only Unity application: Windows PC VR and standalone Quest 2 / Quest 3 (Android ARM64 / OpenXR). Private school/classroom APK distribution is the current release target. The Unity Editor uses the XR Device Simulator for headset/controller testing; it is not a desktop game mode.

## Users
Medical students learning advanced adult thoracic procedural anatomy through hands-on activities and formative assessments. Easy and Medium scaffold entry into independent Hard practice.

## Product Purpose
Teach anatomical relationships, disease mechanisms and six defined treatment pathways through direct interaction with a source-attributed 3D specimen.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Exactly four learning modes: Anatomy Cases, Identify & Enumerate, Assemble Anatomy and Guided Dissection. Free dissection is a sandbox within Guided Dissection. Exactly Easy, Medium and Hard. Complete the VR control introduction to open Easy. Pass Easy to unlock Medium in that activity; pass Medium to unlock Hard. Guest access follows the same locks. Pass at 80 percent plus all essential objectives. Unlimited retries, no compulsory countdown. English narration and captions. Fictional medical learning career, never a qualification.

Six cases: pneumothorax/chest drain; asthma/inhaler and spacer; coronary disease/balloon and stent; pneumonia/prescribed care; pleural effusion/aspiration; aortic stenosis/catheter valve replacement. Detailed instrument events and source-backed content, not arbitrary completion buttons. Case physiology is an educational model, not clinical prediction.

Cases include physical 3D mechanism matching and response prediction. Pleural aspiration adds a symptom-triggered safety decision. Wrong decisions produce specific rationales; an unsafe safety decision prevents an assessment pass. Hard and assessment procedure steps offer instrument alternatives. Learning rewards require demonstrated unassisted mastery; no reward follows from reading text or repeating an already mastered objective.

Supabase email codes, trusted offline PIN, account-isolated atomic saves, idempotent sync and reset generations. Cloud service and physical-device acceptance require actual connected infrastructure and hardware.

## Brand Commitments
Professional charcoal/navy UI, restrained teal, warm neutral text, Source Sans 3; dim lab with neutral specimen lighting. Review the running Unity UI first. One clear next action and one coached activity step at a time; advanced tools and detailed text live on separate pages. Compact repositionable world-space panels. The intact skin, reconstructed fat/fascia and source muscles remain present until an authored layer reveal. Preserve inner anatomy after an appropriate cut or layer exposure.

## Performance
Five presets: Low, Medium, High, Very High, Extreme. User receives suggestions only; never silently reduce quality. Automatic Quest refresh requests the highest rate reported by the headset runtime; users may choose a supported manual rate. No fixed 72 FPS cap. A requested refresh rate is not achieved frame rate; sustained timing and thermal stability require measurements on each target headset. Full settings and platform-specific named profiles.

## Evidence and Source Integrity
Continue the current ThoraxLab assets and native cutting protections. Preserve raw sources and canonical healthy anatomy. Existing reports are historical evidence, not acceptance of new functionality. Geometry marked reference-only is not tissue. Display source and reconstruction attribution.

## Accessibility
Text and captions scale; long names wrap; scroll overflow; non-color-only cues; reduced motion; left/right dominant hand; seated/standing VR; comfort options. Easy assembly uses nearby parts and a matching placement guide. Assessment assistance is explicit and cannot silently reveal answers. Case sequences use cited research without requiring external professional validation for implementation; no claim of clinical validation or certification follows from that decision.

The shared Guide button opens ten illustrated, narrated control and learning steps over the current screen. Close returns to the same activity and reopening remembers the guide page. It blocks specimen interaction while open and never submits assessment evidence. English offline narration uses a female synthetic educational guide; manual audio pause survives help and focus interruptions.
